Farming Strats — Route For Solo Sigs 87

Last updated: Oct 20, 2025•Read time: 4–5 min

Overview

The advantage this strat is the need of less monsters to kill (quest bug). That allows us to use another path for farming.

Contents 1. Core idea 2. Video 3. Analysis 4. Value per Hour

1. Core idea

The Quest Bug gives double amount of killed mobs when farming without group. That means we only need 3 Big monsters and 5 Small monsters of each type.

We only need now less "skeletons" to kill and we can take a different route from the usual. We will take the quest; go to Canyon by main road and kill 3-5 packs of Liberators; then jump the cliff facing south and enter Holy Place; group and kill 3 packs of "skeletons"; jump again the cliff facing the Swamplands; group and kill 3 packs of Dragons; give quest and start again.

Check Video for Details.

2. Video

Rod 36 21 without reckless.

You are free to group as many packs you want in Canyon, even just 3. While the grouping strat in Holy Place is Optimized. The last grouping can be achieved in different ways, just try to not fall from the mount.

3. Analysis

  • With a 36 21 rod you can make 18+ runs/hour
  • in addition to the quest rewards you can get 230 silvers of loot + trash salesman per run
  • You also drop doc81, green herbs, tears and rewind shards
We have a greater Value w.r.t. group farming with an average group thanks to the loot drop and items.
