Briefing

This dossier consolidates efficient farming & grinding patterns. It favors predictable profit, low travel dead-time, and quick decision rules. Use the calculator to adapt to your server’s live prices.

Protocol: track → model → iterate. If variance spikes, downshift to a safer route.

Operating Principles

  • EV per hour beats single lucky drops—rank routes on expected value, not anecdotes.
  • Dead-time matters: seconds spent traveling are seconds not rolling for value.
  • Guardrails: define a budget‑stop before upgrading or gambles.
  • Iteration: change one variable at a time; log outcomes.
